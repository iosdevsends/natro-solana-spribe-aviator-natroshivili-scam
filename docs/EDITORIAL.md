# Editorial guidelines

This document records the tone rules that **every** piece of content in the case file follows, including reader-submitted stories that pass moderation.

## Voice
- **Third-person factual** throughout the case file's own narration. Avoid "I" / "we" outside of quoted statements.
- **Quotes are sacred.** Reproduce verbatim with attribution. Source quotes that contain profanity ("stfu", "Fucking scam") stay verbatim — they are evidence, not the file's voice.
- **No hyperbole.** "98% collapse" not "catastrophic." "Refunds refused" not "betrayed."
- **Pending framing** for unverified claims. "On-chain audit in progress." Not "they rugged."

## Phrases that work
- "Documented case in reputation pricing"
- "What was promised / what was delivered"
- "The scrub" (evidence-removal pattern)
- "On the record"
- "Named parties — self-doxxed by the launch"

## Phrases the case file does not use in its own voice
- "Scam." (Acceptable when quoted from a holder; not as the file's conclusion.)
- "Rug pull" as a conclusion. The on-chain evidence is suggestive, not proven — language is "pending forensics".
- "They stole" / "they robbed". Use "refused to refund" / "took offline" / "removed."
- Any insult against any named party, even when factually justified.

## Right of reply (structural)
The footer's right-of-reply block is non-decorative. Any named party who disputes a claim has that response appended verbatim with date. Errors of fact are corrected promptly.

## Reader-submitted stories — moderation checklist

For each pending submission, the editor checks:

1. **Defamation risk** — does it accuse a named party of a specific crime without evidence the file can stand behind? If yes → request edits or reject.
2. **Factual basis** — are claims linked to primary sources (on-chain, screenshots, archives)? Self-reported figures must be labelled `Self-reported, unverified`.
3. **Verifiability** — on-chain claims require a wallet address that can be independently checked.
4. **Scope** — is the story about a similar reputation-based launch? Off-topic submissions are politely declined.
5. **Tone** — does the submission read as editorial documentation, not personal venting? Editors can suggest tone edits; authors retain the right to refuse and withdraw the submission.

Approved stories are locked once published. Edits to a published story require re-review.

## Translation rules

- EN and RU are the source-of-truth tones, written or reviewed by the editor.
- KA, FR, DE and ES ship as **drafts pending native review**. Editorial supervision still applies; a draft translation must not change meaning relative to EN.
- Russian prison-slang and crude insults are not translated into other languages even when present in screenshots. The screenshot itself is the evidence; a translation into the file's own voice would change the speaker.
