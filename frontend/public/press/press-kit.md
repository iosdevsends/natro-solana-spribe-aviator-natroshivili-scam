# The NATRO File — Press Kit

**Version 1.0 · 28 May 2026 · Public release**

A consolidated reference for journalists, regulators, and counsel covering the
$NATRO Solana memecoin matter.

---

## TL;DR

On 21 May 2026, Alex Natroshvili (15) launched a Solana memecoin called
**$NATRO** on Pump.fun. The marketing leaned on the Natroshvili family name
and the Spribe / Aviator gambling-product association. In the 24 hours before
the launch, **David Natroshvili** (founder & CEO of Spribe) personally
solicited paid presale investment from his verified Instagram, setting a
**$1,500 minimum entry** and routing contact to his **minor son's personal
Telegram**.

Within 48 hours the price collapsed ~98%. Refunds refused. The team admin's
response: "Nothing to say." The founder's personal reply: "stfu." Within 72
hours the website was offline, the founder's Instagram bio link was removed,
and the paid promotional video was deleted.

A documented case file with 25 primary-source exhibits is at **natro.meme**.

## What this is — and isn't

**Is:**
- An investigative case file compiled from primary sources only
- Released under press-ethics standards: third-person factual, with right of
  reply, no scam/fraud conclusions drawn in the file's voice
- Multilingual: EN, RU, UK, KA, FR, DE, ES, AR

**Isn't:**
- A grievance site or a personal complaint
- A claim about Aviator (a separately licensed gambling product) or Spribe
  corporate as an entity
- A claim of contract-level rug-pull — the Pump.fun bonding curve graduated
  and LP was burned; the collapse was driven by holder selling in an open AMM
  *after* the team refused to engage with affected holders
